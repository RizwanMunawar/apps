About Redact for Mac

Redact hides people, faces, licence plates and any other object its model can detect — in a live camera feed, a video file, or a still image. Every frame is processed on the Mac itself: no upload, no cloud, no API key, nothing to leak.

  • Pixelate or blur, by bounding box or by the object's own outline
  • Choose exactly which classes to hide, or import your own model
  • Record the app while it works, for review and demos
